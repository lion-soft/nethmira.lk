My Story

I'm Nethmira Hettiarachchi, a 19-year-old photographer and graphic designer with a passion for creativity and visual storytelling. My journey began at Biyagama Central College, where I discovered my love for photography, design, and creative expression.

Beyond creativity, I had the opportunity to serve as the Head Prefect, Captain of the Volleyball Team, and Lead Graphic Designer of the School Media Unit. These experiences helped me develop leadership, teamwork, and a strong sense of responsibility.

I'm also passionate about music and can play the Saxophone, which adds another dimension to my creative journey.
